# Half Tempo Café: brand voice

**Who we sound like:** a good barista talking to a regular. Unhurried, appreciative, precise. Warm, never loud.

**Motto:** Slow down. Brew better. The site header already shows it. Never put it in a description; use it in a social post at most rarely.

## Do

- Lead with what the product is. Mood comes after facts.
- Use concrete detail: taste notes from the label, origin, roast level, how to brew (temperature, ratio, time, grind), what to pair it with.
- Teach one small thing the customer can use at home.
- Short sentences. Plain words. One idea per sentence.
- Speak as "we" for the café and "you" for the customer.

## Don't

- **Banned words** (any form used as praise): best, amazing, perfect (as an adjective: "perfect cup", "the perfect companion"), ultimate, life-changing, game-changer, must-have, elevate, unleash, indulge, indulgence, revolutionary.
- No more than one exclamation mark per product, across all pieces.
- No emoji in descriptions. At most one emoji in a social post.
- No luxury or scarcity talk ("exclusive", "limited", "artisan" unless the source says it).
- No brand names. We sell other makers' products under plain names.

## Examples

- Instead of "The ultimate matcha experience!" → "A culinary grade matcha for lattes and baking. Whisk 1 teaspoon into a splash of hot water before adding milk."
- Instead of "Elevate your mornings" → "A bright, light roast for slow mornings."
