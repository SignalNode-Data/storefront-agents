# Half Tempo Café: store facts

These are the only facts about the store itself. Agents may state them as written. Anything about the store that is not here (prices other than class prices, shipping times, stock, warranties, guarantees, locations, staff names) must not be stated.

Half Tempo Café is a fictional shop. It sells other makers' products under plain names. It does not roast, blend, grow or make any product it sells.

## The café

- **Name:** Half Tempo Café
- **Line:** Coffee · Tea · Matcha
- **Motto:** Slow down. Brew better.
- **Classes line:** Learn to brew from our baristas.
- **Hours:** open daily, 7 AM to 6 PM.
- **Music:** jazz on vinyl, played low.
- **Space:** quiet tables with power outlets. Laptops are welcome.
- **Decaf:** decaf coffee is always on the bar.
- **Teaching:** our baristas teach every class.

## Classes

All classes: up to 8 people, all tools and tastings included, $25 per class.

| Class | When | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| Pour-Over Basics | Saturdays, 10 AM | Brew pour-over coffee with a gooseneck kettle and freshly ground whole beans |
| Matcha Whisking 101 | Sundays, 11 AM | Whisk matcha by hand; whisks and bowls are provided |
| Milk at Home | Wednesdays, 5 PM | Froth milk for lattes and matcha lattes at home |
| Grind Size Lab | Thursdays, 5 PM | Brew one coffee at five grind sizes and taste the difference |
| Chai From Scratch | Sundays, 4 PM | Make masala chai on the stove |
| Pairing Hour | Fridays, 4 PM | Taste chocolate with coffee and honey with tea |

## Promises

1. **Free brew help at the bar.** Bring anything you bought from us, and a barista will help you dial in your recipe.
2. **Free in-store pickup** for online orders.
3. **Returns:** unopened items can be returned within 30 days. Opened food and drink cannot be returned.
4. **Free class seat with brewing gear:** buy any brewing gear (kettle, grinder or milk frother) and get one free seat in Pour-Over Basics or Matcha Whisking 101.
