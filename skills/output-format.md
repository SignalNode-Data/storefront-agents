# Output format

## Description (product page)

- 80-150 words.
- **Sentence 1 states the product type and the size or quantity** (e.g. "A 12-ounce bag of whole bean, light roast coffee from Rwanda."). If the sources give no size, say what it is and leave the size out; never guess one.
- Include **one concrete, correct brewing or use tip** (temperature, ratio, steep or brew time, grind, whisking) or **one pairing**. Prefer a tip from the research notes or the label.
- End with at most one line about the café (a class, brew help, pickup), taken word for word from store facts.

## Launch brief

- `who_its_for`: one line.
- `hook`: one line. Names one real class or pairing from store facts or the catalog.
- `social_post`: at most 200 characters. At most one emoji.

## Alt-text

- At most 125 characters.
- Describe what is visible: product type, form, packaging, color.
- Do not start with "Image of" or "Picture of".
- **Never name a brand or read out logo text.** Do not claim anything that is not visible.
